# Complete Coordinate Transformation: Rotation + Translation

This guide demonstrates combining rotation and translation for full 2D coordinate transformations.

---

## **Real-Time Scenario: Mobile Robot Following a Path**

Imagine Robot A (reference point) and Robot B (target robot) moving with both position and rotation changes.

---

## **Time: t = 0 seconds**

```
Warehouse Layout:

        Y-axis (North)
          ↑
          |
    ┌─────●────→ X-axis (East)    Robot A
    |    θ_A=0°
    |
    |
    |         ↗ Robot B
    |        (●) θ_B=45°
    |
    └───────────
```

**Robot A (Reference Frame):**

- Position: $(x_A = 0, y_A = 0)$
- Heading: $\theta_A = 0^\circ$ (facing East)

**Robot B (Target):**

- Position: $(x_B = 3, y_B = 2)$
- Heading: $\theta_B = 45^\circ$ (facing Northeast)

**Transformation Calculation:**

$$T_x = x_B - x_A = 3 - 0 = 3$$
$$T_y = y_B - y_A = 2 - 0 = 2$$
$$\theta = \theta_B - \theta_A = 45^\circ - 0^\circ = 45^\circ$$

**Combined Transformation Matrix (Homogeneous Coordinates):**

$$T = \begin{pmatrix} \cos 45^\circ & -\sin 45^\circ & 0 \\ \sin 45^\circ & \cos 45^\circ & 0 \\ T_x & T_y & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0.7071 & -0.7071 & 0 \\ 0.7071 & 0.7071 & 0 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

## **Time: t = 5 seconds**

Both robots move and rotate.

```
Warehouse:

Robot A moves to (2, 1) and rotates to 30°
Robot B moves to (6, 4) and rotates to 75°

        Y-axis
          ↑
    ┌─────●────→ Robot A (at 2,1) facing 30° ↗
    |    (↗)
    |
    |         ↗ Robot B
    |        (●) at (6,4) facing 75° ↗↑
    |
    └───────────
```

**Updated Positions:**

- Robot A: $(x_A = 2, y_A = 1)$, $\theta_A = 30^\circ$
- Robot B: $(x_B = 6, y_B = 4)$, $\theta_B = 75^\circ$

**New Transformation:**

$$T_x = 6 - 2 = 4, \quad T_y = 4 - 1 = 3, \quad \theta = 75^\circ - 30^\circ = 45^\circ$$

## **Key Concepts**

### **Homogeneous Transformation Matrix**

Combines rotation and translation:

$$\begin{pmatrix} x' \\ y' \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & T_x \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & T_y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

### **Applications**

- **Robotics:** Frame transformations between robot links
- **Computer Vision:** Camera pose estimation
- **Navigation:** Converting between coordinate systems
