# Rotation in Coordinate Transformation - Simple Example 

Let's understand rotation with a simple example.

---

## **What is Rotation?**

Rotation means turning points around a center point. In coordinate transformation, we rotate the entire coordinate system.

For a point (x, y) rotated by angle θ counterclockwise:

x' = x * cos(θ) - y * sin(θ)  
y' = x * sin(θ) + y * cos(θ)

---

## **Example: Rotate point (3, 4) by 90**

Original point: (3, 4)

θ = 90 = π/2 radians

cos(90) = 0, sin(90) = 1

x' = 3 * 0 - 4 * 1 = -4  
y' = 3 * 1 + 4 * 0 = 3

New point: (-4, 3)

The point moved from first quadrant to second quadrant.

---

## **Key Points**

- Rotation changes the orientation of points.
- Positive θ is counterclockwise.
- The rotation matrix is used to apply the transformation.

This is the basics of rotation in coordinate transformations!
